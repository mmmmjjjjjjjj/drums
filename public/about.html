Max Jaffe expands the boundaries of possibility for composition and collaboration through the drum set. His music draws from many different genres and seeks to break down the boundaries between them. He is part of a cutting edge of drummers that have embraced SUNHOUSE and their Sensory Percussion technology.

Max is a founding member of experimental rock collective JOBS, Amirtha Kidambi's Elder Ones, Leverage Models, and several others. More recently, Jaffe, with Dave Harrington and Patrick Shiroishi, released the fully improvised and critically acclaimed Speak, Moment, as well as the blistering live album Zebulon! In that same year, Jaffe released Reduction of Man, an album reflective of his decade-plus in New York City’s far ranging improvised music community.

He has toured, performed, and recorded with many diverse artists including Wild Up, EDEN, Teen Daze, Chrome Sparks, Steph Richards, Jeff Parker, WAND, Zach Tenorio, M.A. Tiesenga, Nick Reinhart, Normal Love, Nels Cline, Ava Mendoza, Meg Duffy, Greg Uhlmann, Peter Evans, Rubblebucket, Delicate Steve, Cass McCombs, David Wax Museum, Landlady/Adam Schatz, Lina Tullgren, Potted Plant/Zannie Owens, Oberhofer, The Dreebs, Steven Lugerner, Netherlands/Timo Ellis, and many others.

Available for recording sessions, remote tracking, media scoring, and private lessons (Sensory Percussion, Ableton, drum set, composition).
