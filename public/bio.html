Max Jaffe is a drummer, producer, composer, and technologist based in Los Angeles, CA.

His work expands the possibilities of composition and collaboration through the drum set. He is on the cutting edge of drummers that have embraced SUNHOUSE and their Sensory Percussion technology. ::sample him:: In 2024, Jaffe, with Dave Harrington and Patrick Shiroishi, released the fully improvised and critically acclaimed "Speak, Moment", as well as the blistering live album "Zebulon!" In that same year, Jaffe released "Reduction of Man", an album reflective of his decade-plus in New York City’s far ranging improvised music community.

Max is a founding member of experimental rock collective JOBS, Amirtha Kidambi's Elder Ones, Leverage Models, and several others. He has toured, performed, and recorded with many diverse artists including Wild Up, EDEN, Teen Daze, Chrome Sparks, Steph Richards, WAND, Gavin Gamboa, M.A. Tiesenga, Normal Love, Nels Cline, Ava Mendoza, Delicate Steve, Cass McCombs, Rubblebucket, David Wax Museum, Landlady/Adam Schatz, Lina Tullgren, Kid Millions/Man Forever, Brandon Lopez, DARKSIDE, Dave Binney, Nick Reinhart, Zach Tenorio, Miguel Atwood-Ferguson, Jessica Pavone, Chris Williams, Jeff Parker, Nicole McCabe, Logan Kane, Spencer Zahn, Meg Duffy, Greg Uhlmann, Peter Evans, Potted Plant/Zannie Owens, Oberhofer, The Dreebs, Steven Lugerner, Netherlands/Timo Ellis, and many others.

Available for recording sessions, tours/performances, remote tracking, media scoring, Sensory Percussion tutorials, and drum lessons.
