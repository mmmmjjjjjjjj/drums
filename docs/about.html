Max Jaffe expands the boundaries of possibility for composition and collaboration through the drum set. His music draws from many different genres and seeks to break down the boundaries between them. He is part of a cutting edge of drummers that have embraced SUNHOUSE and their Sensory Percussion technology.

He has toured, performed, and recorded with many diverse artists including Wild Up, Jeff Parker, EDEN, Teen Daze, Chrome Sparks, Steph Richards, WAND, M.A. Tiesenga, Nick Reinhart, Normal Love, Nels Cline, Ava Mendoza, Meg Duffy, Greg Uhlmann, Peter Evans, Rubblebucket, Delicate Steve, Cass McCombs, The Dreebs, Adam Schatz/Landlady, Timo Ellis/Netherlands, David Wax Museum, and many others.

In 2024, Jaffe, with Dave Harrington and Patrick Shiroishi, released the fully improvised and critically acclaimed Speak, Moment, as well as the blistering live album Zebulon! In that same year, Jaffe released Reduction of Man, an album reflective of his decade-plus in New York City’s far ranging improvised music community.

He is a founding member of experimental rock collective JOBS, Amirtha Kidambi's Elder Ones, Leverage Models, and several others.

Available for recording sessions, remote tracking, media scoring, and private lessons (Sensory Percussion, Ableton, drum set, composition).
